Privacy Policy - Hermes Mail Handler App

Hermes Mail Handler App is a private tool for the personal use of its owner.

It accesses only the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Calendar) to read, file and record the owner's own documents.

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Data stays in the owner's own Google account and on the owner's own computer.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
